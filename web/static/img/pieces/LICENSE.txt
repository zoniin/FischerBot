The twelve chess pieces in this directory are the "cburnett" set: the drawings
Colin M.L. Burnett made for Wikipedia in 2006 (Wikimedia Commons, user
Cburnett; "Chess klt45.svg" and its companions), as they are distributed with
a great deal of chess software. The files here came with this project and
their markup may have been tidied; the drawings are his.
